About Daniel Wyatt and this service

Last updated 24 November 2026.

CHC Appeals Navigator is run by Daniel Wyatt, trading as CastilliAI. I help families prepare and present NHS Continuing Healthcare funding appeals in England.

My background

I have spent 20 years in finance and complaints work. I have sat on both sides of that fence: dealing with formal complaints, and helping consumers understand their rights when a process feels stacked against them. I now write plain-English guidance and, with this service, help families organise the evidence that an ICB or an independent review will actually look at.

How I work, and how the guidance is checked

Every guide on this site is written in plain English and checked by me before it is published. I review it against the current National Framework and the official Decision Support Tool guidance. Each guide shows a last-reviewed date and links to the government sources it relies on.

Stage 1 reports are also reviewed by me before they are sent to you. Where I use tools to help organise a large set of records, the judgement in the report is mine. I will tell you honestly if I do not think you have a case.

What I am not

I am not a solicitor, a barrister or a registered clinician. This service is independent advocacy and case preparation, not regulated legal advice and not medical advice. I will say so if a legal remedy looks necessary, and explain the options available to you.

Eligibility is always decided by the integrated care board, not by me. Nobody can guarantee a CHC decision.

The trading name

I trade as CastilliAI. CHC Appeals Navigator is one of my services. You can also find me at castilliai.com.
