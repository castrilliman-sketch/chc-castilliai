How we prepare a funding appeal

Last updated 24 November 2026.

The official path, if you disagree with an eligibility decision, is to ask your integrated care board for a local resolution review, and then, if needed, to ask NHS England for an independent review. We do not replace that process. We help you prepare for it, one paid stage at a time.

1. Start with a free check, if you want one

The 10-question check is a simple weighted questionnaire. It is not the official NHS Checklist or Decision Support Tool, and it is not a decision on eligibility. It is only there to help you judge whether a closer look is worthwhile.

2. Stage 1 — evidence review and a written verdict

For a fixed £349 you send the records you already have: care notes, GP and hospital letters, the Checklist or DST if you have it, and the decision letter. We map what is there to the 12 Decision Support Tool domains and send you a written report with a Yes, No or Maybe verdict, after I have reviewed it.

If the verdict is No, you stop, with nothing more to pay. If it is Yes or Maybe, you decide whether to go on to Stage 2.

3. Stage 2 — the local resolution pack

Local resolution is the ICB's own review process. The National Framework requires every ICB to have one, and says concerns should first be discussed clearly, with a written note of that discussion. Stage 2 is the pack for that process: a letter, commentary on the DST, and preparation for a meeting if one is offered.

If your Stage 1 verdict was Maybe, you instruct Stage 2, and the ICB still upholds the refusal, we refund 25% of the Stage 2 fee when we have the final written decision.

4. Later stages, only if you want them

If you remain dissatisfied after local resolution, you can apply to NHS England for an independent review. Stage 3 is preparation and representation for that review. Stage 4 is help with a complaint to the Parliamentary and Health Service Ombudsman. Stage 5 is a look at a past period of care, including after a death.

You are not obliged to buy any later stage. Fees, cancellation and the 14-day cooling-off period are set out in the pricing page and the terms.
