Stage 1 compared with Beacon’s Expert Analysis

Our Stage 1 evidence review and written Yes / No / Maybe verdict is a fixed £349, with no VAT charged (we are not VAT registered). Beacon’s Expert Analysis Service, a written review after a “not eligible” decision plus a follow-up consultation, is published at £1,600 plus VAT (£1,920 including VAT at 20%). The two services have a similar purpose but are not identical in scope: Beacon’s published Expert Analysis is based on the Decision Support Tool and decision paperwork, with additional documents charged at their hourly rate; our Stage 1 reviews the records you upload and includes 12-domain mapping and a verdict. Families can also get free initial information and advice from Beacon’s NHS England-funded advice service.

Sources: Beacon, Our Fees and this page, accessed 24 November 2026 (Europe/London). This is a comparison of those two published fixed fees only, not a general claim that we are cheaper than Beacon.
