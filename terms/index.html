These terms apply when you buy a stage of CHC Appeals Navigator from Daniel Wyatt t/a CastilliAI.

What this service is

CHC Appeals Navigator is an independent advocacy service provided by Daniel Wyatt, trading as CastilliAI. We are not a law firm or solicitors, we do not give legal advice, and we cannot help with litigation. Daniel has 20 years’ experience in UK finance, including complaints handling, debt purchasing, credit control and leading teams. Reports and appeal packs are case preparation under the National Framework. They are not a formal legal opinion and not a medical diagnosis. Eligibility is decided by the ICB, and by NHS England if a case goes to an independent review. Nobody can guarantee that an appeal will be granted.

Fees and VAT

Fees are as published on the pricing page: Stage 0 is free; Stage 1 is £349; Stage 2 is £799; Stage 3 is £1,499; Stage 4 is £399; Stage 5 is £1,499 plus 8% of any recovery. These are fixed prices and no VAT is charged, because we are not VAT registered. You are not obliged to buy a later stage. Payment plans via GoCardless, where offered, are subject to status.

14-day cooling-off period

For a distance contract, you have a 14-day right to cancel under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013. If you ask us to start work during that period and then cancel, we may charge for the work already done, up to the stage fee.

Stage 2 refund if Stage 1 was Maybe

If the Stage 1 report gives a Maybe verdict, you instruct Stage 2, and the ICB's Local Resolution process upholds the refusal, we will refund 25% of the Stage 2 fee when we have received the final written determination.

Your responsibilities

You must be entitled to act for the person whose records you send, and the information you give us must be accurate so far as you know. You remain responsible for meeting any ICB or NHS England deadlines. We are not a law firm and cannot help with litigation.
