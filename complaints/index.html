How to complain

Last updated 24 November 2026.

If you are unhappy with CHC Appeals Navigator, please tell Daniel Wyatt so that it can be put right. This service is run by him personally. There is no other staff member to escalate to.

Stage 1 — tell us

Email danwyatt398@gmail.com with the word “Complaint” in the subject line, and a short account of what went wrong. We will acknowledge the email within two working days and aim to give you a written reply within ten working days.

Stage 2 — a second look

If you are not satisfied with that reply, say so in writing and ask for the complaint to be looked at again. You will receive a further written response within 14 working days.

If you are still unhappy

We are not a law firm, an NHS body or a regulated claims business, so the Legal Ombudsman and the Parliamentary and Health Service Ombudsman do not deal with complaints about this service. Depending on the issue, you may:

  • complain to the Information Commissioner's Office about the way personal information has been handled;
  • get independent consumer advice, for example from Citizens Advice, or consider a claim in the small claims court for a contract dispute.

If your concern is about an NHS eligibility decision rather than about our work, use the ICB's process and, where relevant, NHS England or the Parliamentary and Health Service Ombudsman. Our time limits guide explains that route.

CHC Appeals Navigator is an independent advocacy service provided by Daniel Wyatt, trading as CastilliAI. We are not a law firm or solicitors, we do not give legal advice, and we cannot help with litigation. Daniel has 20 years’ experience in UK finance, including complaints handling, debt purchasing, credit control and leading teams.

Guidance: National Framework for NHS Continuing Healthcare (GOV.UK). This site covers England.
