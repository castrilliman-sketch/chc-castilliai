How we use personal information

Last updated 24 November 2026.

Who we are

CHC Appeals Navigator is a service of Daniel Wyatt t/a CastilliAI. We are the controller of the personal information you give us. Contact: danwyatt398@gmail.com.

This website

The public pages of this site are a plain information site. They set no cookies and run no analytics or advertising trackers. The free eligibility check runs in your browser and does not send your answers to us. Our host (GitHub Pages) may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.

What we collect when you use the paid service

  • Contact and payment details: your name, email, phone and billing details. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we do not see or store your full card number.
  • Eligibility check answers: only if you later choose to send them to us. The on-site check itself does not transmit answers.
  • Case records: documents you upload about your relative, such as care notes, GP and hospital records, assessments and decision letters. This is health information, which is special category data under UK GDPR.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

  • To provide the stage you have bought: contract.
  • To process health information about your relative: your explicit consent, and where relevant the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. You should only upload a relative's records where you are entitled to act for them, for example with their agreement or as their attorney or deputy.
  • To keep accounting records: legal obligation.

Who we share it with

We share information only as needed to run the service: Stripe (payments), our hosting and storage providers, and, when you instruct us, the ICB, NHS England or the Ombudsman as part of your appeal. We never sell your information or use your family's records to train public AI models.

How long we keep it

We keep case records only as long as needed for your case and any follow-on stages, and accounting records for as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your case records at any time, subject to those legal requirements.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Email danwyatt398@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
